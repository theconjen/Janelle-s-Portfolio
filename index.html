Approach

Focus wins.

Startups fail when they try to speak to everyone. I make informed bets on a target audience, aim everything at them, write down what we learn, and adjust.

Position

Find the language your audience already uses. Retire jargon. Say one true thing, clearly, before saying anything else.

Launch

Ship the brand into the world — site, story, social, outreach — and treat the first hundred true fans as the whole market.

Learn

Every channel is an experiment with a hypothesis. Keep what compounds, cut what doesn't, and document the why.
