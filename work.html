01 — Founder

The Connection

The brief

Faith communities needed a digital home that wasn't a generic social feed. I set out to build one — alone.

The work

Designed the full brand system (palette, typography, voice), then built the product end-to-end: a mobile app, web client, and backend. Communities, events, prayer requests, an apologetics library, chat, and microblogs — shipped through the App Store as a solo founder.

The result

1,000+ organic downloads with zero paid acquisition — growth driven entirely by brand, community, and content.

“Built for believers. No ads. No algorithms.” The brand promise — read the founding story in Why I Built The Connection

02 — First Marketing Hire

Bord Labs

The brief

An early-stage AI startup with strong products and no marketing function. Build the brand — and the department — from a blank page.

The work

Company brand strategy and messaging architecture, product positioning, go-to-market planning, partnership strategy, competitive analysis, founder-brand content, and design direction for web and social. Onboarded the company into a leading startup accelerator during week one.

The result

A complete brand and go-to-market foundation — from a 90-day playbook to live positioning, outreach systems, and launch assets.

03 — Marketing Director

Aramaic Broadcasting Network

The brief

A global broadcast network serving a diaspora audience across languages and platforms — grow engagement and support.

The work

Led marketing 2019–2023 (rehired 2025): audience strategy, social content across platforms, paid media, and high-stakes donor relationships that funded the mission.

The result

30% engagement increase and $25K+ in paid media managed — plus major-donor conversations that taught me how to sell a vision one person at a time.
